Q3 Financial Summary

Revenue grew 12% quarter over quarter, driven by strong performance in the
enterprise search segment. Operating margin improved to 18%, and the team
shipped the new full-text search indexing pipeline ahead of schedule.

Key highlights:
- Elasticsearch cluster migrated to the latest major version with zero downtime.
- Average query latency dropped from 340ms to 45ms after the reindex.
- Customer support tickets related to search relevance fell by 60%.
